The major components of the instrument are available for naming. Exclusive donor-directed naming rights are available for significant gifts beyond those outlined here.

Console

The Oberlin Console

Refurbishing of the historic console from Oberlin College Conservatory.

Console

The Full Conservatory Console

Conception, design and construction of the grand Concert Hall console — five premium keyboards, 300 stops, and associated control systems.

Structure

The Organ Case

The visible pipes and structural elements extending across the full width of the concert hall stage — a singular sculptural masterpiece.

Division

The primary division, featuring a variety of the most full-bodied tones in the Conservatory organ.
